ZONA / Sector

ZONA / Sector is a collective of young independent artists, creating a space for creatives with different skills and visions. Our goal is to build a platform where artists can create independent projects based on their own taste and ideas.

We work with private clients on video and photo production, editing, and other creative services. The income generated is fully directed toward funding projects selected and developed by the ZONA team.

Our main idea is independence. We believe that in Georgia it is possible to create and produce work that is completely independent, free, and authentic.

We are looking for independent artists and people who are ready to create something new based on their own vision.
